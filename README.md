# Downtown Toronto Traffic Intelligence

A GIS and machine learning project focused on analyzing traffic congestion, collision hotspots, transportation risk, and future mobility patterns across Downtown Toronto.

This project combines spatial analysis, predictive modeling, and cartographic storytelling to better understand how traffic patterns vary across the city and where future congestion pressures may emerge.

---

## Project Overview

Downtown Toronto experiences complex transportation challenges driven by dense road infrastructure, high traffic volume, signalized intersections, and collision concentration along major corridors.

The goal of this project was to explore how GIS and machine learning techniques can be combined to:
- identify congestion hotspots,
- analyze collision distribution,
- model transportation risk,
- detect spatial traffic clusters,
- and predict future congestion conditions.

Using a 500 m grid-based framework, multiple transportation datasets were spatially integrated and analyzed in ArcGIS Pro to generate a series of predictive and exploratory maps.

## Key Analysis Components

### Traffic Congestion Analysis
A normalized congestion index was created to identify areas experiencing elevated transportation pressure across the downtown core.

### Collision Hotspot Analysis
Traffic collisions were aggregated into grid cells to examine spatial concentration patterns and identify high-density collision corridors.

### Traffic Risk Probability Modeling
A composite traffic risk index was developed using normalized collision density, road hierarchy, and signal distribution variables.

### Machine Learning Predicted Congestion
Predictive modeling techniques were applied to estimate future congestion conditions across Downtown Toronto.

### Traffic Pattern Clustering
Unsupervised clustering methods were used to group areas with similar transportation behavior and congestion characteristics.

### Future Scenario Comparison
Current congestion conditions were compared against predicted future congestion scenarios to identify areas of potential improvement or increased traffic pressure.
